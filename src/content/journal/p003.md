The oysters are the whole problem and the whole point. Best fish on the flat
sit hard against the racks because nothing else will go in there after them,
and the moment you hook one it knows exactly where to go.
You cannot out-pull it. You lean on it early, before it has picked a
direction, and you turn its head. Once it is pointed at the racks you have
already lost it, you just do not know yet.
Lost three there. Landed the fourth. Sixty-one centimetres on the run-out,
big enough that I had to sit down after.
