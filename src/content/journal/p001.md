Took the tinnie down to the flats on the last of the run-out. Water dropping
off the sand and everything in it going with the water. You do not need to
find the fish. You need to find where the water is taking their breakfast,
and wait there.
The lizards sit on the sand and let it come to them. Cast at the edge where
the flat drops away and hop it back slow. Slow. If you can feel the lure
working you are pulling it too fast.
Two today, both off the drop. Nothing on the top of the tide. There is never
anything on the top of the tide.
